# Reporting principles

DEDSEC reports should be conservative and reproducible.

Observations describe what the scanner saw. Candidates describe security-relevant behavior that still needs verification. Verified findings require explicit verification semantics and one or more evidence references.

Severity should describe demonstrated impact rather than the name of a check. Missing hardening headers, fingerprints, unusual ports, and permissive configuration signals should not automatically become verified vulnerabilities.

Reports should preserve negative and failed outcomes because uncertainty and coverage gaps matter during review.
